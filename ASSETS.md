## Final prompt

Create a standalone transparent-background 3D hero illustration for the chosen TOP PANEL ('01 / PLAYFUL STUDIO') of the reference style comparison board. Reference role: style and subject only; ignore middle and bottom panels completely. Output is ONE isolated 3D composition with alpha transparency, no text, no letters, no website, no frame or typography. A large rounded juicy tangerine-orange squircle block with a soft ivory triangular play symbol embossed on the front, leaning slightly clockwise, a thick sculptural cobalt blue loop curling behind it from lower left to upper right, a small golden yellow reward coin with an embossed star leaning at the bottom right, and one flat mint-green starburst tucked behind at the upper right. Sophisticated tactile rubber/clay materials, fine soft microtexture, broad soft studio lighting from upper left, intentional material highlights, beautiful ambient occlusion and contact shadows on the objects. This matches the warm playful premium portfolio style in panel 01, NOT shiny generic neon or chrome. Clear legible silhouettes at mobile size, art-directed balanced overlapping composition, dominant orange play tile in center, each form stays inside frame with 5 percent safety margins, landscape 3:2 shape. Eye-level three-quarter camera view, no visible ground plane, entirely transparent surrounding background including between loop and tile. No extra particles or tiny items. Design intended to float above a warm ivory webpage with black oversized headlines.
